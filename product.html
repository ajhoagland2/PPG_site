The tabletop golf game

Stand it. Flick it. Mark it.

Pen Pad Golf turns a pen and a printed course into a quick round of golf you can play almost anywhere.

How a round works

  1. Start in the tee box.
  2. Stand your pen upright.
  3. Flick toward the hole.
  4. Mark where the pen lands as one stroke.
  5. Continue from the new mark. Lowest score wins.
